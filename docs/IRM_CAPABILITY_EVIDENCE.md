# IRM capability evidence ledger

This ledger is the authority for comparing Fynix Cyber Audit with the broader [ServiceNow Integrated Risk Management portfolio](https://www.servicenow.com/products/integrated-risk-management.html). It separates implemented behavior from roadmap intent and prevents unsupported parity claims.

## Claim policy

A capability may be described as **implemented** only when this ledger points to all applicable product evidence:

1. a persisted domain model and migration;
2. an authorized operator, REST, or MCP interface;
3. an automated behavior test; and
4. operator documentation for workflows that require it.

Code presence alone is not enough to claim portfolio parity. `docs/plans/` is roadmap material, not product evidence. Partial capabilities must be named narrowly. Missing capabilities must not appear in product or sales copy as available.

## Current defensible product claim

Fynix Cyber Audit implements workflows for **risks, controls, audits, findings, evidence, and remediation**. These workflows can support an IRM program, but Fynix does not currently claim full ServiceNow IRM portfolio parity.

## Parity sequence

Work advances by closing one evidence-complete vertical slice at a time:

1. **Risk portfolio foundation** — delivered: enterprise, operational, and technology risks have domain-aware profiles, accountable owners, appetite thresholds, attributable review snapshots, derived state, issues, scoped UI, REST/MCP detail exposure, and exports; enterprise risks add cycle-safe hierarchy, current active residual-score roll-ups, and versioned deterministic likelihood/impact stress scenarios. Stochastic simulation, calibrated probability, correlation/quantitative loss modeling, external telemetry, verified evidence, and automatic remediation remain.
2. **Policy compliance foundation** — delivered: policy obligations, attributable attestations, exceptions, control mappings, due/overdue status, append-only history through product interfaces, operator UI, REST interface, and documentation. Broader regulatory-change and acknowledgement capabilities remain.
3. **Continuous control testing foundation** — delivered: recurring test definitions, operator/REST observations, server-derived threshold results, due state, append-only history through product interfaces, and failed-test findings. External indicator ingestion, background execution, evidence verification, and automatic remediation triggers remain; finding lifecycle is delivered in slice 7.
4. **Operational resilience foundation** — delivered: business services, versioned impact analyses, typed dependency maps, recovery plans, scenario exercises, RTO/RPO measurement, derived readiness, and exercise issues. Automated discovery, telemetry, exercise orchestration, crisis communications, verified evidence, and automatic remediation triggers remain; issue lifecycle is delivered in slice 7.
5. **AI governance foundation** — delivered: AI system inventory, use cases, owners, versioned risk assessments, control/risk mappings, attributable approval snapshots, derived governance state, monitoring reviews, and issues. Automated discovery, regulatory mapping, verified evidence, continuous telemetry, runtime enforcement, and automatic remediation remain; issue lifecycle is delivered in slice 7.
6. **Third-party risk foundation** — delivered: existing vendor due diligence joined to versioned risk assessments, governed third-party risk links, attributable decision snapshots, periodic reviews, derived state, and review issues. External intelligence, fourth-party mapping, verified evidence, automatic reassessment, and automatic remediation remain; issue lifecycle is delivered in slice 7.
7. **Governance issue and remediation lifecycle** — delivered: issues from risk portfolio, third-party risk, AI monitoring, resilience exercises, and continuous control tests register into a shared owner-scoped lifecycle; managers create linked remediation tasks, completed tasks enter verification, independent verifiers close issues, regressions reopen them, and append-only transition snapshots support reporting. Verified evidence, automatic treatment selection/execution, effectiveness telemetry, and automatic closure remain.

Each slice updates this ledger in the same commit as its behavior and tests. Product copy changes only after the row reaches **Implemented**.
